## What it can do

- Present the restoration site, its aerial photos and plans in English and Croatian
- Switch language without reload (?lang=hr)
- Let a visitor choose a cereal, a pulse and an oil on a Koppen-Geiger climate map (tri/)
- Build to a single-file page and deploy to Cloudflare Pages via a workflow

## Ask for it when

- website for the Kukljica restoration land
- pick crops for a climate zone
- show the Ugljan ecosystem project online

## Inputs / Outputs
