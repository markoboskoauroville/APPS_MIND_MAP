## What it can do

- Teach wave physics, decibels, room modes and treatment
- Cover microphones, signal chain, critical listening, mixing order, compression and loudness delivery
- Separate audible, tested-not-audible and never-tested hi-fi claims
- End with a section on what could not be verified
