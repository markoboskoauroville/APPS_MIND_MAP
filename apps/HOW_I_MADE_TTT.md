## What it can do

- Tells the story of building the TTT mini dictation keyboard
- Explains the lineage: Dictate, FlorisBoard, Android keyboard
- Describes what every row on the keyboard screen does
- Shows screenshots of settings, keys, macros and API-key screens
- Publishes as a GitHub Pages site and as BOOK.md
